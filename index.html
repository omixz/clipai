Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes — 5 videos every calendar month, 3 clips each, no card. Free clips have a small "Made with Peakcut" watermark. Pro ($15/mo) is 20 videos, 5 clips each, no watermark and dubbing; Pro Plus ($29/mo) is 50 videos, 8 clips each and your own watermark.

Will clips get cut off mid-sentence?

No. Clips only start and end on sentence boundaries or natural pauses, and every cut is padded slightly so first and last words aren't chopped.

How long does it take?

Usually a few minutes. You'll see the real stage — transcribing, finding moments, rendering — and can ask for an email when it's ready. Paid plans skip ahead of the free queue.

What happens to my video?

It's only used to make your clips. The upload is deleted as soon as clips are rendered; clips are deleted after 24 hours, so download them. You can wipe everything from Settings at any time.

Do unused videos roll over? Do credits expire?

There are no credits to expire. Your allowance resets on the 1st of each month (UTC). If a video fails to process, it doesn't count.

How do I cancel?

Settings → Manage billing. Stripe's portal lets you cancel, switch plans, update your card or download invoices instantly. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I auto-post my clips?

Yes — hit "Auto-post" on any clip: it saves the clip, copies the title and hashtags, and opens Buffer, which can publish to TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts on a schedule (it has a free plan).

Can I use my own computer or GPU?

Yes, on Pro and Pro Plus (or as a standalone add-on). Pair a computer in Settings → Your computers and run the small Peakcut worker on it. Then choose "My computer" when uploading: your PC does the transcription and rendering (on an NVIDIA GPU or Apple Silicon automatically), and those videos don't use your monthly allowance. You can upload from your phone while your PC at home does the work. The worker also runs fully offline with no upload at all.

Can I edit the captions?

Yes — each clip comes with an .srt file. Import it with the clip into CapCut, Premiere, DaVinci Resolve or YouTube Studio.

Can I dub my clips into another language?

Pro plans can dub English videos into Spanish, French or Portuguese — speech is translated and re-voiced, and captions are translated too.
